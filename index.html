BMI Queue Process

UiPath Orhestrator Gemini Canvas - Body Mass Index (BMI) Calculator Website

In UiPath queue terminology, the concepts of Dispatcher and Performer are often used, in transactional process automation. This define how queue items are added and processed.
Dispatcher - Adds items to the queue. Feeds the robot with work to process. Reads input data (e.g., Excel, database, web forms).
Performer - Processes items from the queue.Performs the actual business process on each transaction.
Set Transaction Status to mark each item as: Successful, Failed (with retry if enabled) Business Exception.
Unitlized Gemini Canvas for Rapid Prototyping. The HTML/CSS structure was iterated within the Canvas to visualize how data should be presented to end-users,tested the BMI calculation logic before building the robot's front-end.

Onboarding Validation-Dispatcher-Performer

Power Automate Desktop Power Automate Work Queues

Work Queues in Power Automate Desktop with the Dispatcher/Performer architecture enable scalable and reliable automation by separating task creation from task execution.
The Dispatcher flow adds individual work items to a queue, while the Performer flow processes each item independently, updating its status and handling errors.
This approach improves performance, supports retries for failed tasks, and allows efficient handling of large workloads

During the first two months, Alex signed two contracts, Mary also signed two, Victor signed one, and Peter did not sign any contracts.
Looking at Alex’s results, he achieved two signed contracts with a conversion rate of 8%. However, he stopped making offers and did not attract new customers. Instead of waiting for contracts to close, he should continue calling potential clients and making more offers.
Mary also signed two contracts, but her lead conversion rate is only 33%, compared to Alex’s 62% and Victor’s 70%. This indicates that her weakness is in generating leads, while her conversion through later stages is acceptable. To improve results, she was paired with Victor, who is strong in lead generation but weaker in making offers and closing contracts.
Peter initially appeared unsuccessful since he signed no contracts during the first two months. However, he started contacting clients later because he was assigned to another project. His conversion between stages is strong. After three months, Peter turned out to be the top performer, signing six contracts.
Meanwhile, Alex improved his lead generation to maintain a sustainable pipeline, and Mary and Victor worked together to achieve a balanced conversion performance.

Dataverse Knowledge Integration

Microsoft Copilot Studio Dataverse Knowledge Integration

This demonstrates how to use Microsoft Copilot Studio with Dataverse tables as a structured knowledge source. It showcases how conversational AI can retrieve, interpret, and respond to business data stored in Dataverse (e.g., Account and Contact tables).
Using Copilot Studio’s Knowledge feature, Dataverse tables are connected directly to the copilot, enabling:
1. Structured data retrieval
2. Cross-table querying (Account + Contact)
3. Context-aware conversations
4. Business terminology understanding via column descriptions & glossary
5. Synonym support for flexible user queries

This implementation allows organizations to transform Dataverse data into an intelligent conversational assistant.

Example Queries:
1. Who is our largest account?
2. What is their address?
3. Who is the primary contact for that account?
4. What is her job title?
